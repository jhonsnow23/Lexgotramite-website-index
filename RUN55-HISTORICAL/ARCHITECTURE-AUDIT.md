## Legacy route
GSC → select → research plan with three broad questions → BOE heading/sample + official sources → focused evidence → legal dossier → model research coverage → gap assessor restricted to GENERAL_GUIDANCE (regularización) → matrix and graph → require whole-guide readiness OR require preapproval of one of three alternatives → only then article_request → integrity and independent semantic review → artifacts. A rejection at proposal level never produced a candidate. The same research evidence was repeatedly sent into proposals instead of revising actual draft content. A standalone `answered` label was insufficient and historical retries could fail due to format or scope gates. Prior patches were useful but left ordinary categories subject to these constraints.

## Run 55 route
Manual first-article integration or existing GSC selection → fixed reader question and article type → directly retrieve category-specific primary provisions via actual observed BOE index IDs → supplement registered official guidance and a statutory cross-reference → construct existing literal passage catalog + applicability dossier → model returns proposed article paragraphs, conditions, exceptions, classified gaps and targeted article requests using a closed schema → deterministic provenance check → targeted retrieval when necessary → if no declared material gaps and a coherent paragraph inventory exists, render an explicitly unapproved candidate → separate Astra review of the exact Markdown and structured article, passage mapping, all official evidence and scope → require all checks and all claim IDs, plus prior defects resolved → if veto, retrieve newly requested official articles and repair actual paragraphs; never re-review an unchanged candidate just to get approval → final review-ready files OR continue another authorized seed within budget. The normal workflow retains its selected topic and history; the new bootstrap workflow rotates.

The new engine does not mutate prior research into complete coverage. It explicitly separates claim provenance from semantic legal support. It does NOT run the legacy proposal approval gate, which is not an approval of the actual article. The legacy engine and previous replay tests remain in the repository. The unapproved candidate is assembled directly from model-produced, provenance-linked paragraphs, not from a separately semantically pre-approved inventory; the binding legal validation is of the actual draft. This is a deliberate simplification rather than an assertion that evidence IDs prove legal correctness.

## Decision gates
1. Commercial scope and metadata duplicate guard.
2. Observed BOE index identity and dated version; primary provision required. Official-source URL allowlist. No invented URLs/block IDs.
3. Schema validation, closed passage aliases, provenance/hash/offset verification, applicable consolidated law as legal basis; Article 191 mandatory with its referred requirements in extraordinary continuity. Official guidance alone cannot prove a legal rule.
4. Gap materiality: unresolved material/unknown gaps block; applicant-dependent and resolved-by-evidence must cite evidence. Optional/excluded limitations are reviewed for misleading omissions. Additional articles are retrieved within the limit. Missing personal facts are not missing legislation.
5. Candidate creation is not approval. Explicit title, scope, limitations; citations rendered by Python. No public page changes.
6. All review checks true, all claims assessed exactly once, no defects, all prior defects explicitly resolved, final approved true. A negative criterion, invalid schema, missing checks, or unresolved defect remains blocking.
7. Budget and state checks; rotation if an eligible topic cannot be completed. Separate model responses; no claim of human review or certification.
8. Always publication_allowed=false; production artifact names are emitted only after real model review. Simulation artifacts cannot become review-ready production artifacts.
